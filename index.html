FTC Search Shark helps teams quickly find and understand official FTC game manual rules. Instead of scrolling through hundreds of pages, you can search keywords and instantly get relevant rules.

The FTC rulebook is large and often difficult to navigate during preparation or competition. This tool makes that process faster, clearer, and more accessible.

Our system is designed to improve efficiency, reduce confusion during rule interpretation, and help teams focus more on strategy and robot design. Using no AI, our website is guaranteed to give you 100% accurate information directly from the latest game manual.
